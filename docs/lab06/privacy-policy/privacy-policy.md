# FitFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction
FitFlow is a fitness tracking app developed as a university HCI prototype (IT3060, SLIIT).

## 2. Information We Collect
- Account: name, email, age
- Fitness: workouts, steps, calories, streaks
- Nutrition: meal photos, food logs
- Community: posts, comments, likes
- Device: OS version, device model

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- To generate personalized workout recommendations
- To display progress and community features

## 4. AI Processing
AI features (Daily Flow, food recognition) use simulated/mock data. No real AI model processes your personal data.

## 5. Data Storage
Data stored locally on device. No server transmission.

## 6. Data Sharing
We do not sell your data.

## 7. Your Rights
- Access your data
- Delete your data
- Withdraw consent
- Contact: fitflow@example.com

## 8. GDPR / CCPA
We aim to comply with GDPR and CCPA standards.

## 9. Health Disclaimer
FitFlow is not a medical device. Not medical advice.

## 10. Children's Privacy
Not for users under 18.
