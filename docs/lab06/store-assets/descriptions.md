## Full Description
FitFlow is your AI-powered fitness companion.

- AI Daily Flow: Personalized workout recommendation every day.
- Workout Planner: Review, customize, and start your training plan.
- Progress Tracking: Weekly activity, streaks, and achievements.
- Community: Private circles and challenges for accountability.
- Nutrition Logger: Camera-first meal logging.

Note: This is a university HCI prototype. AI recommendations and food recognition use simulated data. Firebase and backend are not yet implemented.
